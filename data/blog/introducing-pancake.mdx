# The Pancake Ecosystem — Overview + Deep Dive into Pancake Multi-Channel Chat

> **TL;DR:** Pancake **isn't a single piece of software** — it's an **ecosystem of 7 products** covering the entire multi-channel sales lifecycle: **Fintab** (accounting), **Botcake** (AI chatbot), **Pancake POS** (multi-channel sales), **PANCAKE** (multi-channel chat — flagship product), **Pancake CRM** (customer data), **WEBCAKE** (web + landing pages), **Pancake Work** (internal management). This article gives a complete overview of the ecosystem + a deep dive into **Pancake multi-channel chat** — the flagship product trusted by 300,000+ Vietnamese businesses, handling 15 billion+ messages and 250 million+ orders per year.

In today's reality where shops must be present on **Facebook, Instagram, Zalo, TikTok, Shopee, Lazada, WhatsApp, and Website** simultaneously, a single piece of software can't cover every operational aspect. Pancake solves this by **splitting into 7 specialized products** — each one excellent at its domain, all connected through shared data + workflows.

---

## 1. The Pancake ecosystem — 7 products

![Pancake ecosystem with 7 products: Fintab, Botcake, Pancake POS, PANCAKE, Pancake CRM, WEBCAKE, Pancake Work](/static/images/blog/pancake-ecosystem/ecosystem-diagram.jpg)

| Product | Function | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| **PANCAKE** (multi-channel chat) | Centralized conversation management across Messenger, Instagram, Zalo, TikTok, WhatsApp, Web chat | Any shop whose customers message via FB/IG/Zalo |
| **Pancake POS** | Multi-channel sales + inventory sync with Shopee/Lazada/TikTok Shop | Shops with physical products needing order + shipping management |
| **Pancake CRM** | Lead/contact/order/ticket pipeline + automation + Conversion API | B2B/B2C sales teams needing funnel + long-term care |
| **Botcake** | AI-powered chatbot — auto-reply, drip campaigns, remarketing | High-volume teams needing 24/7 auto-reply |
| **WEBCAKE** | SEO-friendly Website + Landing page builder | Building campaign landing pages, syncing to POS/CRM |
| **Fintab** | Accounting software for SMEs + small businesses | Companies needing financial reports + reconciliation |
| **Pancake Work** | Internal management software (tasks, time tracking, HR) | Scaling teams needing internal admin separate from sales |

Each product has its **own dashboard, separate pricing plan, separate API**, but they're designed to **connect seamlessly** — e.g. a chat in PANCAKE → customer replies → Botcake fires automation → order created in Pancake POS → lead saved to CRM → accountant reconciles via Fintab.

## 2. The sales conversion funnel — how 7 products work together

Pancake's ecosystem is designed around the standard **sales conversion funnel** — each stage has 1-3 suitable products:

![Sales conversion funnel showing which Pancake products serve each stage](/static/images/blog/pancake-ecosystem/funnel.jpg)

| Stage | Primary products | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Discovery** | PANCAKE, WEBCAKE, Botcake, Pancake POS | Customers find you via chat ads / landing pages / chatbots |
| **2. Consideration** | PANCAKE, Botcake, Pancake POS | Customers message asking about price; bot/sales advises |
| **3. Purchase** | PANCAKE, Botcake, Pancake POS, Pancake CRM | Create order, collect payment, save lead to CRM |
| **4. Re-engagement** | Botcake, Pancake CRM | Drip campaigns + automation for post-sale care |
| **5. Internal operations** | Pancake Work | Team management of tasks, time tracking, reports |

**Implication**: you don't need all 7 products from day one — start with what fits your business stage, expand as needed. Most shops start with **PANCAKE chat + Pancake POS**, then add CRM + Botcake as they scale.

## 5. Pancake multi-channel chat (flagship product)

From this section onward, the article focuses on **PANCAKE** (multi-channel chat) — the first and largest product in the ecosystem.

### 5.1. What is Pancake chat?

**Pancake chat** is a **multi-channel conversation management solution** — consolidating messages from:

- **Facebook Messenger**
- **Instagram Direct**
- **WhatsApp Business**
- **Zalo OA**
- **TikTok**
- **Telegram**
- **Web chat** (livechat)
- **Shopee / Lazada chat**

→ into **a single interface**, so agents don't have to keep 9 tabs open.

### 5.2. 4 core features

![Pancake chat's 4 core features: multi-channel conversation management, livechat + assignment, conversation tags, filtering](/static/images/blog/pancake-ecosystem/chat-core-features.jpg)

**① Multi-channel conversation management**
Integrates + syncs conversations from Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp, Zalo, TikTok... in one interface. Realtime sync — if a customer messages on FB at 14:23, the agent sees it instantly on the Pancake dashboard.

**② Livechat + conversation assignment**
Auto-assigns conversations to staff (built-in round-robin — see [the tag/assign API post](/en/blog/pancake-conversation-workflow-tag-assign-api)), ensuring consistent conversation frequency + quality. Comes with rich livechat features for websites.

**③ Conversation tags**
Categorize customers via a tag system — sync data to Ads Manager in realtime for retargeting. Example: tag "Asked price but didn't buy" → Pancake pushes them into a Facebook Custom Audience → run retargeting.

**④ Conversation filters**
Multi-dimensional filtering: by post source, by ad ID, by time of most recent interaction, by customers who left a phone number... Sales can quickly find conversations needing follow-up today.

### 5.3. Advanced features

#### Business Calling — call via WhatsApp/Messenger from inside Pancake

![Business Calling — call customers directly via WhatsApp/Messenger from the Pancake interface](/static/images/blog/pancake-ecosystem/business-calling.jpg)

Pancake integrates **direct calling** to customers via WhatsApp or Messenger right from the chat interface. Agents don't have to switch apps; call + chat history stays in one place.

> [!TIP]
> This is especially valuable for **export / cross-border** shops — international customers primarily use WhatsApp and can't be called via a regular Vietnamese phone number.

#### Integrated call center

![Integrated call center + call-stats dashboard inside Pancake](/static/images/blog/pancake-ecosystem/call-center.jpg)

The **"Call Center"** section provides detailed statistics:

- **Call volume over time** — total inbound/outbound calls across a time range
- **Total calls + talk time** — inbound / outbound / successful / averages
- **Successful call rate by agent** — % of successful calls per agent
- **Top 5 agents by successful calls** — KPI ranking

→ This data can be pulled via API to integrate with internal BI dashboards (Looker Studio, Metabase, Grafana).

#### Pancake AI assistant

![Pancake AI assistant: suggested replies, product suggestions from images, customer sentiment detection](/static/images/blog/pancake-ecosystem/pancake-ai.jpg)

**3 core capabilities of Pancake AI**:

- **AI quick reply suggestions** — based on conversation context, AI proposes the most appropriate reply instantly
- **Product suggestions from images** — when a customer sends a photo of clothing or a product, AI automatically suggests similar in-stock items
- **Customer sentiment detection** — AI recognizes sentiment (happy, frustrated, hesitant) in realtime based on conversation history

→ Helps agents **reply faster + more accurately**, reducing per-conversation handling time by 30-50% for high-volume shops.

### 5.4. Flexible chat models — POS or CRM integration

![Flexible chat models: chat + order creation (POS) and chat + customer info (CRM)](/static/images/blog/pancake-ecosystem/chat-model-flex.jpg)

Pancake chat operates in **two models** depending on your business vertical:

**Model A — Pancake chat + POS** (physical-product shops)
- Integrates with POS to **auto-create orders** during the chat itself
- Order history is tied to the conversation
- Fits: fashion, cosmetics, F&B, electronics

**Model B — Pancake chat + CRM** (service industries)
- Integrates with CRM for **long-term customer care** + service-industry data
- Fits: spa, healthcare, real estate, travel, education

→ You don't have to pick just one model — Pancake supports running both in parallel if your business has multiple verticals.

### 5.5. Management dashboard + reporting

![Pancake management dashboard with CRM integration, direct order creation, customer tagging, fanpage stats](/static/images/blog/pancake-ecosystem/chat-management-ui.jpg)

The interface is designed to be **user-friendly + optimized for teams of 5-50+ agents**:

- CRM integration for customer data management
- Create orders directly from Pancake, store customer data in the conversation
- Customer tagging, categorization + quick reply templates
- Fanpage stats + per-agent performance tracking (new phone numbers, re-engaged conversations, new customers...)
